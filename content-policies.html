Content Policies

Quality Standards

All resources submitted to IPL must meet our quality standards:

  • Content must be educational and genuinely useful
  • Resources should be actively maintained and up-to-date
  • Links must point to legitimate, safe websites
  • Content must be accessible (ideally free or freemium)

Prohibited Content

The following types of content are not allowed:

  • Spam or promotional content without educational value
  • Malware, phishing, or harmful websites
  • Pirated or illegally distributed content
  • Hate speech or discriminatory content
  • Content that violates copyright laws

Review Process

All submitted resources are reviewed by community moderators before being published. Reviews typically take 1-3 business days. Resources may be rejected if they don't meet our standards, with feedback provided.

Appeals

If your submission was rejected and you believe it meets our standards, you can appeal by contacting us at appeals@ipl.org.
