Cookie Policy

Last updated: December 2024

What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your device when you visit a website. They help websites remember information about your visit, like your preferred language and other settings.

How We Use Cookies

IPL uses a minimal number of cookies to ensure the website functions properly:

Cookie Purpose Duration
preferred_language Remembers your language selection 1 year
session_id Maintains your logged-in session Session

Managing Cookies

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to refuse or delete cookies. However, disabling cookies may affect some features of the website.
