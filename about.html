About Internet Public Library

The Free Encyclopedia of Curated Resources

The Internet Public Library (IPL) is a free, community-driven directory of curated educational resources. Our mission is to organize the world's best learning materials and make them accessible to everyone, everywhere.

Our Story

IPL was born from a simple observation: the internet has an abundance of amazing educational content, but finding high-quality resources can be overwhelming. Traditional search engines return millions of results, making it hard to distinguish the exceptional from the mediocre.

We set out to create a curated directory where every resource has been reviewed for quality, accuracy, and educational value. Like a library staffed by expert librarians, IPL helps you find exactly what you need to learn.

Today, IPL is maintained by a global community of contributors who share our passion for accessible education. We're open source, ad-free, and committed to remaining free forever.
