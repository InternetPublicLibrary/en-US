Contribution Guidelines

Quality First: Only submit resources that are high-quality, well-maintained, and genuinely useful.

Free Resources: We prioritize free educational resources, though paid resources with exceptional value may be included.

Accurate Categories: Place resources in the most appropriate category to help users find them.

Be Respectful: Follow our code of conduct and be respectful in all interactions.
