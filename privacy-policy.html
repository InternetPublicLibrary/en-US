Privacy Policy

Last updated: December 2024

Information We Collect

  • Account Info: Email and name if you create an account
  • Usage Data: Anonymous analytics about site usage

How We Use Information

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We only share data with service providers for hosting and analytics, or when required by law.

Your Rights

  • Request data correction
  • Request account deletion
  • Export your data
