Our Mission

To organize the world's best educational resources and make them freely accessible to everyone.

Global Access

Curated Quality

Community Driven

Why We Exist

The internet contains overwhelming amounts of educational content. Finding high-quality resources amidst the noise is challenging. IPL solves this by curating only the best materials across every subject.

We believe access to quality education is a fundamental right. By organizing and freely sharing resources, we empower learners everywhere.
