Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Internet Public Library?

The Internet Public Library (IPL) is a free, community-driven directory of curated educational resources. Our mission is to organize the world's best learning materials and make them accessible to everyone.

How do I find resources?

You can browse resources by category, use the search function, or explore random resources. Each resource includes a description, rating, and direct link to the original content.

How can I contribute?

Visit our Contribute page to learn about the many ways you can help - from submitting new resources to translating content.

Is IPL really free?

Yes! IPL is completely free to use. We're an open-source project maintained by volunteers from around the world. No subscriptions, no ads, no paywalls.
