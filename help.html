Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Internet Public Library?

IPL is a free, curated directory of educational resources organized by category. Think of it as a library for the internet's best learning materials.

How can I contribute?

You can submit new resources, report broken links, suggest improvements, or help translate content.

Is IPL really free?

Yes! IPL is completely free to use. We're an open-source project maintained by volunteers.

How do I report an issue?

Use our issue reporting form or open an issue on GitHub.
